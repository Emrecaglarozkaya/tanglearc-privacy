Need help?

If TangleArc is not working as expected, please open a support request using the link above. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version/build, and a short description of the issue.

Common questions

  • Hints: TangleArc starts with a small number of free hints. Additional rewarded hints require a rewarded ad to complete successfully.
  • Ads: Banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads are supplied through Google AdMob. Availability can vary by region, device, consent status, and ad inventory.
  • Progress: Gameplay progress and preferences are primarily stored locally on the device.
  • Privacy choices: Where required, TangleArc presents privacy or consent controls through Google's User Messaging Platform.
